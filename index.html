The Cartesian Plane

The x-axis runs left–right. The y-axis runs up–down. They cross at the origin (0, 0). An ordered pair (x, y) says: move x across first, then y up (positive) or down (negative). Negative x means left; negative y means down.

Ordered Pairs in the Classroom

Seats are named by row and column. To use coordinates we choose an origin and a rule. Our rule: x = column counted from the left, y = row counted from the front (bottom of the picture). The front-left seat is (1, 1). Juan sits at (1, 1) and Juana at (3, 2).
